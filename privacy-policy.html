Optixia Media Player — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Controller: Stuart David Harrison, trading as Optixia, Am Obersthof 35, 52538 Selfkant, Germany, stuart.h57.sh@gmail.com

Summary

Optixia Media Player is a player. It has no accounts of its own, no advertising and no analytics. We do not receive, store or sell your data: what you add stays on your device. The app does talk directly to services you choose to use (your playlist provider, Trakt, OpenSubtitles). This page lists everything that leaves your device. This version of the app is for Android TV.

What stays on your device

  • Playlist details you enter (server addresses, usernames, passwords, playlist URLs or files)
  • The channel, film, series and TV-guide data downloaded from your provider
  • Favourites, watch history, resume positions, reminders, recordings, watchlist and settings

This information stays on your device. It is deleted when you remove a playlist, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Backup files you export are saved where you choose and are under your control.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

  • Your playlist provider. The app sends your provider the login or playlist address you entered, and asks it for your playlist, TV guide and streams. We never see any of this. Your provider's own privacy policy applies. Some providers only offer unencrypted (HTTP) connections. If yours is one of them, what the app sends it, including your login, is not encrypted on the way. Use an https address if your provider offers one.
  • Trakt (charts and title details). To load the Top 20 charts, film and series information, artwork, trailers and cast, the app asks Trakt for public information. These requests contain title IDs and search terms, not personal information. Trakt sees your IP address, as with any web request. See Trakt's privacy policy. Charts, title details and artwork are kept on your device for 7 days so they are not requested again.
  • Trakt (your account, optional). Only if you connect your Trakt account in Settings › Trakt. The app then sends Trakt the films and episodes you play (title IDs and how far through you are), adds and removes titles on your Trakt watchlist, and reads your Trakt watch history and watchlist. Your Trakt sign-in token is stored only on your device. Signing out stops this and revokes the token.
  • OpenSubtitles (optional). Only when you choose Download subtitles in the player. The app sends the title (or its TMDB ID), year, season and episode and your chosen language, then downloads the file you pick. No account details are sent; OpenSubtitles sees your IP address. See OpenSubtitles' privacy policy.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB), if enabled. Weekly charts and artwork, with no personal information. TMDB sees your IP address, as with any web request.
  • Google Play. Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google, who tell us whether a purchase is active. We never see your payment details. If you share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may give us anonymous crash and performance reports (Android vitals). See Google's privacy policy.
  • Voice search (optional). Only when you press the microphone in Search and allow the microphone permission. The app listens only while the box says "Listening…". Your device's own speech service (on Google TV and Android, Google's) turns what you say into text, which may involve sending the audio to that service's servers. The app receives only the resulting text, uses it as the search, and never records or stores your voice. You can decline the permission and type instead. See your speech service's privacy policy.
  • Google Cast (not used on Android TV). The app contains Google's Chromecast software, which is reserved for a future phone and tablet version and is never started on a TV, so casting does not happen in this version. When it is switched on in a later version, that software will send anonymous information to Google about how casting is used, and the stream address (which can include your provider login) and the title and artwork will go to the Cast device you choose. This page will be updated before that happens. See Google's privacy policy.
  • YouTube. Only if you tap "Trailer", which opens the YouTube app or website.

What we do not do

We do not run servers that receive your data, show advertising, include advertising or analytics code of our own, build profiles, or sell or share your information with advertisers.

Security

Everything the app stores stays on your device, protected by your device's own security. Sign-in tokens for Trakt are stored only on your device. Connections to Trakt, OpenSubtitles, TMDB and Google use HTTPS.

Deleting your data

We hold none of your data on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete. You can remove it yourself at any time: - Playlists and what came with them: Settings › Playlists › Delete removes a playlist and its channels, films, series and guide data from the device. - Everything the app stores: Android Settings › Apps › Optixia Media Player › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the app. - Trakt: Settings › Trakt › Sign out in the app stops the sharing and revokes the token. To delete your history on Trakt, use your Trakt account settings. - Your playlist provider and OpenSubtitles: contact them to delete data they hold. - Questions or requests: stuart.h57.sh@gmail.com.

Children

Optixia Media Player is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
